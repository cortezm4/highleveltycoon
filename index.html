About

When businesses come to me, it's usually because something in their sales process is broken. Leads fill out a form and don't hear back for hours. The CRM is a pile of disconnected tools that don't talk to each other. The website looks like it was thrown together years ago and doesn't match the quality of the business behind it. Sometimes it's all three at once.

I fix that by building the whole system around how the business actually runs. That means setting up GoHighLevel properly, pipelines, funnels, calendars, and connecting it to whatever else you're using through APIs and webhooks so nothing sits in its own silo. It means putting an AI voice agent or chatbot in place so calls and messages get answered the moment they come in, not hours later. It means writing the follow up sequences, the texts, the emails, the calls, so a lead never just sits there waiting to be forgotten. And when the website itself needs work, I design or redesign it on GoHighLevel or whatever CRM you already run on, so the whole thing finally looks and functions the way the business deserves.

25 projects in, the result clients keep telling me about is the same one every time. Faster response times, fewer leads slipping through the cracks, more appointments actually landing on the calendar, and a system that keeps working without anyone having to babysit it every day.

I'm Oluwatimileyin, a GoHighLevel Specialist with 3+ years doing exactly this.
